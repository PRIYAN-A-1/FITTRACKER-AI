# FitTrack product direction

Functional reference: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.snapcalorie.alpha002 . Used to understand the category's photograph/review/log flow, not as a layout or asset template.

FitTrack uses a calm ivory surface, forest green actions, clay energy accents, white rounded journal cards and a plate/leaf icon. The screens use everyday nutrition language instead of neon AI branding or a chatbot dashboard.

Distinctive implemented priorities: transparent food source/version, unknown-value handling, measured portion confirmation, private local photo processing, explicit uncertain recognition, account-scoped offline drafts and correction by selecting the right catalog record. These are design/implementation choices, not a claim that no competing app has similar features.

The app remains Android-only. No separate website is included. Workout generation and fitness-coach features remain excluded.
